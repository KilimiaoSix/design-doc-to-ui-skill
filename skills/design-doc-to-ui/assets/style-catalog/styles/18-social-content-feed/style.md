# 18 Social Content Feed

Sample: `../../generated-samples/18-social-content-feed-style-board.png`

## Style Summary

Social content feed UI is media-led, fast-scanning, and interaction-heavy. It balances creator identity, content previews, reactions, comments, follow actions, and platform navigation without letting the feed become visually chaotic.

## Visual Language

- White and soft gray surfaces with black text, coral reactions, blue actions, violet highlights, and green online/status dots.
- Media cards, avatar rows, follow buttons, reaction controls, comment chips, and bottom navigation.
- Clear content hierarchy: creator, media, reaction/action row, comments or related content.
- Wide layouts support sidebars and management surfaces; mobile focuses on vertical feed rhythm.
- Image and video previews should look real but remain bounded by app structure.

## Four-Endpoint Guidance

- Web: show discovery feed, left navigation, creator rows, and sidebar modules.
- PC: support creator/feed management with media grid, comments, analytics hints, and posting controls.
- Mobile: prioritize vertical feed cards, reactions, bottom nav, and quick creation action.
- Tablet: use split feed and comment/detail layout with expanded media previews.

## Prompt Guidance

Ask for a creator or social content app with feed cards, avatars, media thumbnails, reactions, comments, follows, and mobile bottom navigation. Keep usernames and body text as placeholders to avoid fake or unreadable copy.

## Negative Constraints

Avoid random usernames, meme-poster layouts, overstuffed feeds, illegible comments, fake social branding, broken media frames, and duplicated device screens.

## Accessibility Risks

Reaction-only controls need labels. Image-heavy feeds require contrast protection over media, clear focus states, and reduced-motion alternatives for live/video content.
