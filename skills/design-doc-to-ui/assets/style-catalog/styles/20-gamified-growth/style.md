# 20 Gamified Growth

Sample: `../../generated-samples/20-gamified-growth-style-board.png`

## Style Summary

Gamified growth UI turns operational goals into progress loops, missions, rewards, streaks, and campaign events. It should feel energetic and motivating while remaining a usable product interface for tracking and action.

## Visual Language

- Deep navy and clean white surfaces with electric blue, lime, orange, magenta, and small gold reward accents.
- XP bars, mission cards, reward chests, streak meters, badges, leaderboard rows, and event panels.
- Small mascot or avatar mark can reinforce reward loops without taking over the workflow.
- Strong, readable progress hierarchy with clear action buttons.
- Dark surfaces are useful for energy but should be balanced with high-contrast content panels.

## Four-Endpoint Guidance

- Web: show goals, campaign panels, missions, XP, and rewards in a dashboard layout.
- PC: support mission-control workflows with leaderboard, task queue, active missions, and metrics.
- Mobile: prioritize daily quests, streak, reward, and one clear start/claim action.
- Tablet: use expanded event map or rewards board with missions and progress.

## Prompt Guidance

Ask for a growth or operations app with XP, missions, rewards, streaks, leaderboards, and event campaigns. Keep it recognizably product UI, not a fantasy game poster, by requiring navigation, controls, cards, and measurable progress.

## Negative Constraints

Avoid game-poster composition, unreadable neon, fake fantasy words, overdecorated mascots, low-contrast dark text, excessive particle effects, and duplicated layouts.

## Accessibility Risks

High-energy palettes can create contrast and cognitive-load issues. Rewards, streaks, and leaderboards should be optional or carefully framed to avoid manipulative pressure, and progress states must not rely on color alone.
