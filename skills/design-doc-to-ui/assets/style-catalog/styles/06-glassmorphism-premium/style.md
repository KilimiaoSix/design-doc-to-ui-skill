## Intent

Premium glassmorphism for portal, finance-lite, account, membership, and executive overview apps that need depth and a refined feel without becoming decorative.

## Visual Language

- Frosted translucent panels over dark atmospheric backgrounds.
- Thin luminous borders, soft blur, layered depth, restrained reflections.
- Deep charcoal or midnight blue base with cyan and warm gold accents.
- Dense enough for real dashboards, but with generous panel spacing and strong section hierarchy.

## Endpoint Adaptation

- Web: browser frame, sidebar navigation, account cards, balance trend, action panel, and activity list.
- PC: denser analytics workspace with charts, asset allocation, tables, and controls.
- Mobile: stacked account summary with bottom navigation and large quick actions.
- Tablet: split dashboard with navigation rail, goals, financial metrics, and forecast panels.

## Prompt Guidance

Ask for a four-panel board with Web, PC, Mobile, and Tablet shown side by side. Keep UI text short, use real product structures, and specify frosted translucent overlays, layered blur, thin borders, and restrained premium accents.

## Negative Constraints

Avoid pure poster art, oversized marketing copy, decorative glass blobs, unreadable transparent text, brand logos, and excessive glow that hides the interface.

## Risks

- Low contrast is the main risk; production use needs contrast tokens and solid fallback surfaces.
- Heavy blur and transparency can hurt performance on low-end devices.
- Finance-style layouts require extra care around precision, error states, and accessible chart legends.
