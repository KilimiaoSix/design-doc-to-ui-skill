# 17 Commerce Retail

Sample: `../../generated-samples/17-commerce-retail-style-board.png`

## Style Summary

Commerce retail UI is product-first, conversion-oriented, and operationally clear. It highlights product imagery, filtering, offers, cart state, price clarity, and direct purchase actions while keeping the path to checkout obvious.

## Visual Language

- White and warm gray surfaces with black text, cobalt CTAs, red sale accents, green status accents, and small yellow promo highlights.
- Product cards with strong imagery, price chips, sale badges, filter controls, and cart affordances.
- Clear hierarchy for browse, compare, product detail, cart, and checkout states.
- Restrained decoration so product imagery remains the main visual asset.
- Dense but scannable layouts on wide endpoints, simplified purchase flow on mobile.

## Four-Endpoint Guidance

- Web: show storefront discovery, hero/product feature, filters, and product grid.
- PC: support catalog browsing with persistent filters, product grid, and cart preview.
- Mobile: prioritize product detail, variant selection, price, quantity, and sticky buy action.
- Tablet: use split catalog and cart/checkout layout with touch-friendly product cards.

## Prompt Guidance

Ask for an e-commerce product with product tiles, filters, price chips, sale badges, cart preview, and checkout CTA. Product images should be real-looking and the interface should feel transactional, not like a marketing campaign page.

## Negative Constraints

Avoid ad posters, long fake product names, brand logos, unreadable prices, fake checkout legal text, oversized hero-only screens, and duplicated layouts.

## Accessibility Risks

Sale red and CTA blue need contrast validation. Price, discount, inventory, and error states must use text plus visual indicators, not color alone.
