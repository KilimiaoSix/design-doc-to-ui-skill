# 11 Skeuomorphic Realistic

Sample: `../../generated-samples/11-skeuomorphic-realistic-style-board.png`

## Style Summary

Skeuomorphic realistic UI uses physical material metaphors to make digital controls feel tactile and familiar. It works best for focused tools where the interface benefits from a sense of craft, such as audio production, maker tools, collectibles, retro finance, or premium niche utilities.

## Visual Language

- Realistic textures: walnut wood, dark leather, brushed metal, glass, enamel, rubber, and inset surfaces.
- Physical controls: knobs, sliders, switches, VU meters, recessed buttons, and beveled panels.
- Lighting: soft studio shadows, believable highlights, and clear separation between raised and sunken controls.
- Typography: small functional labels with high contrast against textured surfaces.
- Layout: dense but organized control groups, with clear affordances and direct manipulation.

## Four-Endpoint Guidance

- Web: use a full-width workspace with browser chrome, grouped panels, and larger control clusters.
- PC: support dense desktop workflows with tool panels, precision sliders, and persistent navigation.
- Mobile: simplify to the most important controls; keep touch targets large despite the realistic styling.
- Tablet: combine desktop-like density with touch-friendly spacing and split regions.

## Prompt Guidance

Ask for a real app UI, not a hardware product photo. Specify the same product scenario across Web, PC, Mobile, and Tablet. Use material terms directly: leather grain, brushed metal, glass meters, walnut trim, inset controls, beveled panels, and tactile sliders.

## Negative Constraints

Avoid poster layouts, pure hardware shots, unreadable tiny labels, excessive nostalgia, low-contrast text on texture, and controls that look decorative rather than interactive.

## Accessibility Risks

Textured surfaces and realistic lighting can reduce contrast. Dense physical controls can become too small on mobile. The style needs explicit contrast checks, large touch targets, and simplified responsive variants.
