# 12 Editorial Magazine

Sample: `../../generated-samples/12-editorial-magazine-style-board.png`

## Style Summary

Editorial magazine UI uses publication-grade composition for content-rich products. It emphasizes strong typographic hierarchy, image-led storytelling, precise grids, and pacing that feels closer to a premium magazine than a generic feed.

## Visual Language

- Large serif headlines paired with restrained sans-serif UI labels.
- Full-bleed photography, curated image crops, and article cards.
- Ivory paper surfaces, charcoal text, thin divider rules, and muted accent color.
- Strong grid systems with asymmetry used carefully for feature stories.
- Calm whitespace and deliberate section rhythm.

## Four-Endpoint Guidance

- Web: lead with a large feature story and a structured editorial grid.
- PC: add persistent side navigation and richer issue or collection browsing.
- Mobile: stack the story flow while preserving headline hierarchy and image quality.
- Tablet: use expanded editorial spreads with side-by-side feature and picker modules.

## Prompt Guidance

Specify an actual content product with navigation, article cards, saved items, and issue structure. Ask for image-led app screens rather than magazine covers. Keep text short and readable.

## Negative Constraints

Avoid pure cover art, decorative collages, overlong headlines, poster-only layouts, unreadable caption blocks, and generic blog templates.

## Accessibility Risks

Large photography can compete with text. Serif headlines need careful sizing on mobile. Muted palettes require contrast checks for captions and secondary controls.
