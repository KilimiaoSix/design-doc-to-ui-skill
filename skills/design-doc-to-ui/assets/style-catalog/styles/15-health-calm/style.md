# 15 Health Calm

Sample: `../../generated-samples/15-health-calm-style-board.png`

## Style Summary

Health calm UI supports healthcare and wellness workflows with a reassuring, low-stress interface. It uses soft colors, direct labels, large touch targets, and clear status indicators while preserving clinical clarity.

## Visual Language

- Soft blue, sage green, white, warm off-white, charcoal text, and limited coral alert color.
- Rounded cards, spacious form fields, simple line icons, and gentle progress rings.
- High contrast for core medical data and clear hierarchy for urgent states.
- Friendly but professional imagery or avatars used sparingly.
- Calm spacing and predictable navigation.

## Four-Endpoint Guidance

- Web: show care overview, visits, vitals, meds, and messaging in a dashboard layout.
- PC: support care coordination with larger cards and persistent navigation.
- Mobile: prioritize today's visit, vitals, meds, and direct check-in or message actions.
- Tablet: use expanded care-plan and vitals layouts with touch-friendly controls.

## Prompt Guidance

Ask for a healthcare or wellness coordination product with appointments, care plan, vitals, medication reminders, symptom check-in, and secure messaging. Use soft clinical language and avoid fear-based imagery.

## Negative Constraints

Avoid hospital-stock-photo posters, low contrast pastel text, alarming imagery, crowded forms, fake medical claims, and tiny medication details.

## Accessibility Risks

Pastel palettes can fail contrast checks. Medical data must remain readable and should not rely on icons alone. Emotion indicators should supplement, not replace, explicit labels.
