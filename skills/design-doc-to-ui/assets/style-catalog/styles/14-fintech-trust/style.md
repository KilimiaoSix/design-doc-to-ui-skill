# 14 Fintech Trust

Sample: `../../generated-samples/14-fintech-trust-style-board.png`

## Style Summary

Fintech trust UI prioritizes clarity, stability, and risk awareness. It should look conservative enough for money movement while still feeling modern and efficient. The main pattern is precise financial data inside crisp, accessible structures.

## Visual Language

- Deep navy foundations with white cards and cool gray dividers.
- Trustworthy green for positive deltas and restrained amber for risk or review states.
- Clear charts: allocation rings, cash-flow bars, trend lines, and compact metric cards.
- Icon-led navigation with minimal labels to reduce text error risk.
- Strong number hierarchy and predictable spacing.

## Four-Endpoint Guidance

- Web: show dashboard-level balance, portfolio, cash, risk, and payment modules.
- PC: support denser analytics with the same card system and navigation rail.
- Mobile: stack cards in priority order and keep critical actions visible.
- Tablet: combine overview charts with transaction or account lists.

## Prompt Guidance

Ask for a trusted financial planning or banking analytics product. Specify balances, portfolio allocation, cash flow, risk alerts, and payments. Keep text sparse and rely on charts, numbers, icons, and semantic color.

## Negative Constraints

Avoid flashy crypto styling, gaming or casino cues, speculative neon palettes, crowded microcopy, and illegible transaction tables.

## Accessibility Risks

Do not rely on color alone for gains, losses, or risk. Financial numbers need high contrast and stable alignment. Small chart labels may need alternate table views.
